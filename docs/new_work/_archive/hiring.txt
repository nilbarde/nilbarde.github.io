Story Draft: Team Expansion and Onboarding Excellence

I joined in August 2022. When I first joined the company, the deployment team did not yet exist. For about a year, my colleague and I handled all deployment and maintenance tasks ourselves. Over time, we recognized that we could rely on less experienced engineers for these tasks, relying on experienced engineers wasn't cost-effective and could impact the quality of team's output.

About a year after my arrival, we began forming the deployment team, we initially hired one engineer to handle these tasks. I took on a central role in its expansion. With help of the HR, I collaborated with external hiring agency TopHire. Initial screening process was handled by TopHire, I gave them guidelines and skills to look out for in the candidates. I took technical interviews of candidates shortlisted by TopHire. For the candidates who qualified after technical interview, I designed an assignment for them closely related to their JD and work they will be going to do in the company, if they get hired. This helped us in getting best view on how candidates will contribute to the work. The candidates with good assignment and technical interview, qualified for final HR-cum-tech interview with Engineering Manager. Engineering manager was happy with both candidates I shortlisted for the interview. Finally I got to have the say to whom to hire and who to keep on hold, I made the decision based on who had better chemistry with the deployment engineer we already had in the team, as they were going to work together and if they work good together we were going to get the best output. For contract negotiation, I had a call with TopHire team with HR present on the call, I explained them terms of hiring, probation period, salary before and after probation. HR helped me to fill in gaps whenever needed as it was my first time explaining contract. The candidate was happy with offered contract and terms, so it was simple process.

After few months, with influx of more projects, we needed more engineers. This time we had hired internal hiring manager Gopal. I directly worked with him to shortlist candidates who applied through LinkedIn and also we searched for engineers who matched our JD and contacted them. With prior experience of hiring, I updated initial screening questions and Gopal did excellent work on shortlisting candidates based on those. Almost all candidates got qualified for the next round. After assignment and it's review round, I got to filter out few candidates. Then again same Engineering Manager interview. Out of selected 2 candidates, was candidate was fresher, so we had to modify our policies and explain him additional terms to ensure that his inexperience won't cause any problems to the team dynamics. For contract negotiation, Gopal helped me in the process, but he let me lead the calls and gave me feedback after every call.

Few months later, with extra projects, we decided to hire 2 more engineers. So I worked with consulting agency for the hiring process. Here the screening questions helped again and consulting team contacted applicants shortlisted by me. 

One significant challenge was aligning salary expectations with our budget. For one candidate, whose expectations were double our initial budget, I engaged in multiple discussions to provide market insights and highlight the career growth opportunities within our company. This ultimately helped us reach a mutually beneficial agreement.

Additionally, as we onboarded new engineers at different times, I advocated for thorough documentation of our processes. Although this initially increased the workload, it ultimately streamlined the onboarding process and reduced the burden on our team.

This journey not only strengthened our team but also showcased my commitment to efficiency and growth.
