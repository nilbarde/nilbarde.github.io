## 2. The Current Inflection Point

Derq is restructuring at the same time Nihal is reaching for the next level. Three things are happening simultaneously:

1. **Strategic shift:** the company is moving from "client-acquisition-first" to "product-first." Custom client work is being pushed to convert into reusable product capability.
2. **Org shift:** function-based teams (software, ML, FS) are being dissolved into product-based teams.
3. **Personal placement:** Nihal is most likely to be placed on the **analytics team** in the new structure, with senior people from other departments likely to lead it.

**What this means for him:**

- Strategically, analytics fits his strengths (analytical rigor, fast development, ML integration depth).
- Politically, he can't credibly compete for the analytics team-lead role against more senior peers — and explicitly doesn't want to displace them.
- His real ambition — product ownership, client exposure, decision-making input, engineering leadership *across* modules — has to be earned through influence rather than title.

This is a textbook **"earn the role by doing the role"** moment. He's actively pursuing a promotion that formalizes contributions he's already making, while the organizational floor is being repainted underneath him.
