## ✍️ WRITING FORMULA

**Opening Hook** (1 sentence)
- Start with action or surprising fact
- ❌ "Throughout my career, I have always been passionate about..."
- ✅ "When our ML model reduced manufacturing scrap by 50%, exceeding client expectations, they signed €5M in new contracts."

**Context** (1-2 sentences)
- Essential background only
- ❌ Long history of how you got there
- ✅ Minimum needed to understand the challenge

**Action** (2-3 sentences)
- What YOU did (use "I" not "we")
- Specific actions, not vague descriptions
- ❌ "I was responsible for the project"
- ✅ "I interviewed 25 engineers across 8 countries to identify pain points"

**Result** (1-2 sentences)
- Quantify everything possible
- Business impact > technical achievement
- ❌ "The project was successful"
- ✅ "Secured €5M in contracts and reduced deployment time by 90%"

**Learning/Reflection** (1-2 sentences)
- What you learned
- How you'll apply it (connect to MBA/future goals)
- ❌ "This taught me leadership skills"
- ✅ "This showed me that technology creates value only when aligned with business outcomes—a principle I'll deepen through [School's] strategy curriculum"

## 🚫 COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. **Too much technical detail**
   - ❌ "I used a convolutional neural network with ResNet-50 architecture..."
   - ✅ "I developed computer vision models that improved accuracy by 30%"

2. **Using "we" instead of "I"**
   - ❌ "We secured funding..."
   - ✅ "I secured ₹30L in funding by..."

3. **Listing responsibilities instead of achievements**
   - ❌ "I was responsible for managing the team"
   - ✅ "I led a team of 10, delivering results that won bronze and silver medals"

4. **No numbers**
   - ❌ "The project was very successful"
   - ✅ "The project generated €5M in contracts"

5. **No connection to future**
   - ❌ Story ends with result
   - ✅ Story ends with "This experience revealed my gap in [X], which [School's] [Y] will address"

## 💡 FINAL TIPS

**Word Economy**:
- Cut every unnecessary word
- Short words > long words
- Active voice > passive voice

**Show Don't Tell**:
- ❌ "I am a strong leader"
- ✅ "Led 10-person team to bronze and silver medals"

**Specificity**:
- ❌ "Many countries"
- ✅ "8 countries across 4 continents"

**Impact Over Process**:
- ❌ "I conducted extensive research and many interviews..."
- ✅ "Research across 25 engineers revealed 3 critical pain points, which guided our redesign"

**Connect Everything to Goals**:
- Every story should answer: "So what?" and "Why does this matter for MBA?"
