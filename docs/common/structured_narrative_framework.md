## Essay Writing Principles

### Structure Every Story with STAR+I
- **Situation**: Set context (briefly)
- **Task**: What challenge/goal
- **Action**: What YOU did (use "I" not "we", be specific)
- **Result**: Quantify impact
- **Insight**: What you learned / How you grew

### Show Don't Tell
❌ "I am a strong leader"
✅ "Selected from 75 candidates to lead inter-IIT team, secured bronze & silver medals"

### Quantify Everything
- ₹30L funding secured
- 50% scrap reduction
- €144k annual savings
- €5M contracts
- 10 weeks → 1 week deployment
- 8 countries
- 75 → 10 candidate selection

### Connect to Future Goals
Every story should have a "so what" that ties to:
- Why you need MBA
- What you'll do after
- How it serves your long-term vision

## Red Flags to Address

### Potential Concerns
1. **Why leave technical work?** → Not leaving tech, building ON it to create business impact
2. **GPA: 7.04/10** → Strong but not exceptional. Counter with: achievements, competitions, extracurriculars
3. **Why MBA vs. MS?** → Already have deep tech expertise; need business frameworks
4. **Career switching risk** → Not switching; natural evolution from tech → tech PM → tech entrepreneur

### How to Address
- Emphasize PROGRESSION not PIVOT
- Show PM experience already validates transition
- Demonstrate business acumen through Tvarit impact
- Frame MBA as amplifier, not course correction

## Writing Tips

### Voice & Tone
- Confident but humble
- Results-focused but reflective
- Specific but concise
- Passionate about impact, not just technology

### Word Economy
- Cut: "In order to" → "To"
- Cut: "I was responsible for" → "I led"
- Cut: "very, really, extremely" → Show magnitude through facts
- Cut: Opening throat-clearing → Start with action/hook

### Vary Your Examples
- Don't use self-driving car for EVERY essay
- Spread different modules across different essays
- Show breadth of experience

### End Strong
Every essay should end with forward momentum:
- What you learned
- How you'll apply it
- Why it matters for your future
