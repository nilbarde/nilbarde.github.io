## STORYTELLING IMPROVEMENTS V2 → V3

### **1. Lead with Emotion, Not Logic**:
- ❌ V2: "Helping others overcome struggles..." (values statement)
- ✅ V3: "Football has always been a source of powerful emotions..." (emotion + action)

### **2. Show Agency in Difficult Choices**:
- ❌ V2: "After securing admission... I lost my spot"
- ✅ V3: "I cleared entrance exams for IIT, BITS and VIT... rather than settling for BITS or VIT, I took a drop year"

### **3. Frame Weaknesses as Context, Not Excuses**:
- ❌ V2: Not explicitly addressed
- ✅ V3: "Scholarships was the only way to keep up with advantaged students from urban areas"

### **4. Add Humility to Achievement Stories**:
- ❌ V2: Prof. Sethi mentorship mentioned factually
- ✅ V3: "I foolishly presumed it was because of my skill set... gradually dawned on me there was more to learn"

### **5. Close with Power, Not Summary**:
- ❌ V2: "These qualities allow me to contribute..."
- ✅ V3: "I never stopped dreaming. I never let setbacks define me. I refused to believe my origins could set boundaries for me."

## STYLE GUIDE FROM V3

### **Openings**:
✅ Emotion + action: "Football has always been a source of powerful emotions"
✅ Context first: "Growing up, performing well... was the only way to keep up"
✅ Specific detail: "As a 15-year-old, away from home for the first time"

### **Body Paragraphs**:
✅ Show humility: "I foolishly presumed..."
✅ Connect to present: "which helps me professionally to date"
✅ Quantify: "$750,000 cash reward"

### **Closings**:
✅ Power statements: "I never stopped dreaming. I never let setbacks define me."
✅ Forward-looking: "with London as my next potential meaningful chapter"
✅ Values reaffirmation: "true fulfilment comes from challenging yourself and helping others"
