OPPORTUNITY & RESEARCH PHASE

Upon joining Derq, leveraging my expertise in robotics and product development from IIT Bombay's self-driving car project, I was given the opportunity to lead the development of a new product aimed at improving the safety of vulnerable road users through blinking traffic signs.

The challenge was complex: how do you get the attention of pedestrians, cyclists, and other vulnerable road users in high-traffic areas where distraction is common and accidents frequently occur? The solution couldn't just be technically sound—it needed to be grounded in actual user behavior.

My first step was conducting extensive research to determine how different types of road users respond to variations in:
- **Traffic sign colors** (which colors command attention without causing confusion?)
- **Blink frequency** (how fast should signs blink to be noticed but not ignored?)
- **Intensity/brightness** (what luminosity levels work in different lighting conditions?)
- **Time of day** (do requirements differ between daylight, dusk, and nighttime?)

This wasn't just literature review—it involved analyzing accident data, studying existing signaling systems, and understanding the psychology of attention and perception in traffic environments.

PRODUCT PLANNING & STAKEHOLDER BUY-IN

Based on these research insights, I developed a comprehensive product plan that outlined:
- Technical approach (hardware + software + ML integration)
- User behavior insights justifying design choices
- Implementation timeline and resource requirements
- Success metrics and validation approach
- Risk mitigation strategies

I presented this plan to senior management, articulating not just what we would build but why this approach would work where generic solutions might fail. The presentation required translating technical complexity into business value—a skill I was still developing at the time.

Senior management approved the project, and I was given full ownership to lead development.

CROSS-FUNCTIONAL PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT

After receiving approval, I led the product development process by delegating tasks across multiple teams while maintaining overall coordination:

**Machine Learning Team:**
- Tasked with developing predictive models to assess vulnerable road user behavior in real-time
- Models needed to detect pedestrians, cyclists, wheelchair users
- Predict trajectory and determine when blinking signs should activate
- Balance false positives (unnecessary activations) vs. false negatives (missed warnings)

**Hardware Selection (My Responsibility):**
- Evaluated multiple communication devices to identify optimal solution
- Key criteria: reliability, weather resistance, power consumption, maintenance requirements
- Needed compatibility with existing traffic infrastructure
- Cost-effectiveness for multi-site deployment
- Negotiated with vendors for favorable terms

**Software Development (My Leadership):**
- Managed design and development of interface between hardware and ML models
- Ensured low latency (warnings must be instant)
- Built fail-safe mechanisms (system must default to safe state if errors occur)
- Created monitoring and diagnostic tools for remote management

**Integration & Testing:**
- Coordinated across teams to ensure seamless integration
- Designed testing protocols that simulated real-world conditions
- Iterated based on early results

PROTOTYPE & DEPLOYMENT

The deployment process went smoothly, and the product met all client specifications.
