JOINING TVARIT GMBH - DUAL ROLE OPPORTUNITY

A couple of weeks after taking my final semester exams, I joined Tvarit GmbH as a Machine Learning Engineer. Tvarit GmbH focuses on reducing manufacturing scrap using AI. Founded in 2018, the start-up offered me the role of Product Manager alongside my primary role one month after joining, based on my strong one-on-one communication skills. I worked with two part-time Product Management consultants and one Product Management advisor.

MARKET RESEARCH & INITIAL DISCOVERY

My journey began with conducting market research and reviewing the SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures) of manufacturing plants. This research helped me understand the current landscape and identify opportunities for product development. I then visited a manufacturing plant to conduct user persona interviews with engineers working in production and decision-makers involved in financial choices. Based on this research, I proposed initial suggestions on which processes could be targeted to save costs and reduce human effort.

PRODUCT STRATEGY & WIREFRAME DEVELOPMENT

Next, I worked with the product management team to define the Product Strategy and Product Roadmap. We collaborated on creating basic wireframes and UX designs for the product. Once the wireframe was completed, we reviewed it with relevant stakeholders. After receiving feedback, we iterated on the wireframes, implementing necessary changes. This process was repeated with another round of reviews, where feedback was gathered from engineers in plants across eight countries (Mexico, Brazil, South Africa, Germany, Italy, Czech Republic, Turkey, and India), each with different requirements and personas.

MULTI-COUNTRY CUSTOMIZATION & USER TESTING

After understanding the different customization needs across regions, we updated the wireframes and UX designs again. We then began User Testing using the redesigned interface. With the help of a consultant, I designed tasks that each user persona would perform on the product to ensure usability. I took note of pain points users experienced while navigating the product and identified opportunities for improvements, such as adding a help button and simplifying task completion.

ML MODEL ENHANCEMENT & DEPLOYMENT SUCCESS

During the interviews, I also collected valuable technical data that contributed to improving our machine-learning model. I collaborated with a PhD graduate to analyze current features, identify missing parameters, and enhance the model. We successfully deployed the model at a manufacturing plant in South Africa, leading to a 50% reduction in scrap and an increase in annual earnings by €144,000. This success exceeded the client's expectations, securing us contracts worth over €5 million and accelerating ongoing projects.

DEPLOYMENT PIPELINE INNOVATION & PATENT

Using insights from interviews, I developed a new deployment pipeline that reduced deployment time from 10 weeks to just 1 week. I also designed easy-to-use input forms to gather more information from clients with minimal effort. Our methodology was patented in two countries. I was awarded the Technical Person of the Quarter and Employee of the Quarter honours for my contributions.

REFLECTION:
Tvarit was my first exposure to translating technical capabilities into business outcomes. Being promoted to Product Manager after just one month validated my ability to communicate with stakeholders and understand their needs. The 8-country feedback process taught me that great products aren't built in isolation—they emerge from deep user understanding and iterative refinement.

The South Africa deployment was a turning point. When our ML model delivered 50% scrap reduction (exceeding the client's expectations), it unlocked €5M in contracts. This taught me that exceptional execution doesn't just satisfy clients—it creates exponential business opportunities. The deployment pipeline innovation (10 weeks → 1 week) showed me that process improvements can be as valuable as product features.

Working with diverse stakeholders across continents revealed gaps in my business knowledge. While I could conduct user research and iterate on designs, I lacked frameworks for:
- Strategic business model design
- Financial analysis & ROI optimization
- Market entry strategies for different regions
- Scaling operations across geographies

These gaps motivated my MBA pursuit.
